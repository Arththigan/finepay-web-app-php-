About FinePay.com

FinePay.com is a modern digital platform designed to help you manage and pay your police traffic fines easily, securely, and efficiently. Developed with the aim of reducing paperwork, queues, and delays, our service empowers citizens to resolve fines from the comfort of their homes.

Our Mission

To provide a secure, transparent, and user-friendly platform for managing and paying police fines online, in collaboration with the Sri Lanka Police Department.

What We Do

  • Enable users to check their outstanding traffic fines
  • Offer secure online payment methods
  • Generate instant digital receipts
  • Ensure user data privacy and legal compliance

Why Choose FinePay?

With FinePay, you can say goodbye to long queues, manual forms, and missed deadlines. Our platform is available 24/7, ensuring you can make payments anytime, anywhere, with just a few clicks.

Security & Trust

We use the latest encryption technology to protect your data and ensure all transactions are processed safely. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.
